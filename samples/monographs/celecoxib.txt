Drug Name: Celecoxib
Molecular Weight: 381.4 g/mol

Indications and Usage:
Celecoxib capsules are indicated for relief of the signs and symptoms of osteoarthritis and of
rheumatoid arthritis in adults.

Dosage and Administration:
Usual dose: 200 mg orally once daily.

Mechanism of Action:
Celecoxib is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug that selectively inhibits cyclooxygenase-2
(COX-2), reducing the synthesis of prostaglandins.

Pharmacokinetics:
Peak plasma concentration (Cmax): approximately 705 ng/mL after a single 200 mg dose
Time to peak: approximately 3 hours
Apparent volume of distribution (Vss/F): approximately 400 L
Apparent clearance (CL/F): approximately 500 mL/min
Effective half-life: approximately 11 hours
Protein binding: approximately 97%
