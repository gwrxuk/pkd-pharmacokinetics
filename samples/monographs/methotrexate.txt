Drug Name: Methotrexate
Molecular Weight: 454.4 g/mol

Indications and Usage:
Methotrexate is indicated for the maintenance therapy of acute lymphoblastic leukemia.

Dosage: 15 mg orally once weekly.

Mechanism of Action:
Methotrexate competitively inhibits dihydrofolic acid reductase, the enzyme that reduces folic
acid to tetrahydrofolic acid, thereby interfering with DNA synthesis and cell replication.

Pharmacokinetics:
Oral bioavailability: approximately 60% at doses of 30 mg/m2 or less
Time to peak: 1 to 2 hours
Volume of distribution: 0.4 to 0.8 L/kg
Terminal half-life: 3 to 10 hours
Protein binding: approximately 50%
