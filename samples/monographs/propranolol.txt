Drug Name: Propranolol Hydrochloride
Molecular Weight: 295.8 g/mol

Indications and Usage:
Propranolol is indicated for the management of hypertension and for long-term management of
angina pectoris due to coronary atherosclerosis.

Dosage and Administration:
Usual dose: 40 mg orally twice daily.

Mechanism of Action:
Propranolol is a nonselective beta-adrenergic receptor blocking agent. It competitively blocks
both beta-1 and beta-2 adrenergic receptors, reducing heart rate, myocardial contractility and
renin release.

Pharmacokinetics:
Bioavailability: approximately 25% due to extensive first-pass metabolism
Peak plasma concentration (Cmax): about 35 ng/mL after a 40 mg oral dose
Time to peak: 1 to 2 hours
Volume of distribution: approximately 4 L/kg
Clearance: approximately 1 L/min
Elimination half-life: 3 to 5 hours
Protein binding: approximately 90%
