Drug Name: Sildenafil Citrate
Drug Class: Phosphodiesterase inhibitor
Molecular Weight: 474.6 g/mol (free base)

Indications and Usage:
Sildenafil tablets are indicated for the treatment of erectile dysfunction.

Dosage and Administration:
Recommended dose: 50 mg orally, taken as needed approximately once daily.

Mechanism of Action:
Sildenafil enhances the effect of nitric oxide by inhibiting cyclic guanosine monophosphate
(cGMP)-specific phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5), which is responsible for degradation of cGMP
in the corpus cavernosum.

Clinical Pharmacology - Pharmacokinetics:
Absolute bioavailability: approximately 41%
Time to peak (Tmax): 1 hour (fasted)
Peak plasma concentration (Cmax): approximately 200 ng/mL after a 50 mg dose
Volume of distribution (Vss): 105 L
Total body clearance: 41 L/h
Terminal half-life: approximately 4 hours
Plasma protein binding: 96%
